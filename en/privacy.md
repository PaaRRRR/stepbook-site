Stepbook ("we") operates the Stepbook app (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

## At a glance

- The App has **no accounts**. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
- **Your videos never leave your phone.** We don't send your photo library videos, or videos you make in the App, to our servers or to any other company. We don't run any servers.
- To fix and improve the App, we send only error reports and usage statistics to third-party services (Sentry and Amplitude). These never include videos, file contents, or file names.
- Apple and Google process payments. We never receive your card or other payment details.

## 1. Why we process information

- To find and fix errors in the App
- To understand how the App is used and improve it
- To check your free comparisons and subscription status

## 2. What we process

**A. Sent to third-party services (collected automatically)**

| Type | Items |
|---|---|
| Error reports (Sentry) | Error details and location, time, app version, device model, OS version, a log of in-app actions just before the error |
| Usage statistics (Amplitude) | Actions in the App (e.g. starting a comparison, finishing a save), a random device ID created by the App, device model, OS, language, app version, country and city estimated from your IP address |

- The "random device ID" is a value the App generates the first time it opens. We don't use advertising IDs or hardware identifiers such as IMEI or serial numbers, and we don't link this data with other companies' apps or websites to track you.

**B. Stored only on your phone (never sent to us)**

- Your library list (references to videos in your photo library), BPM and trim positions for each video, settings, and this month's practice record
- Free comparison count and device ID. So that deleting and reinstalling the App doesn't reset your free comparisons, these are stored in this device's Keychain on iPhone, and in your Google account backup (Block Store and app backup) on Android.

**C. Phone permissions**

- Photos and videos: to show videos from your photo library and to save videos you create to it. We never delete or change your originals. You can change this permission in your phone's settings at any time.

**D. Payments**

- Apple App Store and Google Play process subscription payments. On your phone, the App only checks the subscription status the store reports (e.g. active, expiry date).

## 3. How long we keep it

- Error reports: kept for up to 90 days after collection, then deleted automatically
- Usage statistics: kept for up to 12 months after collection, then deleted (or earlier, once the purpose is fulfilled)
- On-phone data: deleted when you delete the App. The free comparison count and device ID remain as described in 2-B. They are removed by resetting the device on iPhone, or by deleting your Google account backup on Android.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

We don't share your information with third parties, except where required by law.

## 5. Service providers and international transfers

We use the service providers below. Your information is transferred outside your country in the process.

| Recipient (contact) | Country | Items | Purpose | When and how | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry · privacy@sentry.io) | United States | Error reports (2-A) | Error analysis | Sent over the internet when an error occurs | As in section 3 |
| Amplitude, Inc. (privacy@amplitude.com) | United States | Usage statistics (2-A) | Usage analysis | Sent over the internet while you use the App | As in section 3 |

- If you don't want this transfer, you can stop using or delete the App, in which case you won't be able to use its features.

## 6. Deletion

We delete information without delay once its retention period ends or its purpose is fulfilled. Data held by service providers is removed through their automatic deletion or on our request. On-phone data is removed when you delete the App.

## 7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your information at any time by contacting us (section 10), and we will respond without delay. Because the App can't identify you by name or account, we handle requests to the extent we can locate your information.

## 8. Children

The App doesn't ask for names or contact details and doesn't knowingly collect information from children under 14.

## 9. Security

- Information sent to service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
- We send only what is needed, and never videos, file contents, or file names.
- Only the operator can access the service providers' dashboards.

- Email: stepbook.app@gmail.com

## 11. Changes

We'll announce changes in the App or on this page 7 days before they take effect, or 30 days before for changes that significantly affect your rights.
